# 🛠️ Universal Life Copilot Skill for Gemini

The single, ultimate "plug-and-play" skill designed to make daily life easier for everyone. 

Whether you are a student, busy parent, creator, manager, or absolute tech beginner, this skill transforms Gemini into a powerful mental filter. It instantly tidies up messy notes, builds clear daily schedules, drafts stress-free emails, and breaks down overwhelming choices into easy, actionable steps.

## 📦 How to Download and Upload This Skill to Gemini

Follow these simple steps to import this skill directly into your Gemini web account using the upload interface:

### Step 1: Download the Files
1. Click the green **Code** button at the top right of this GitHub page.
2. Select **Download ZIP** from the dropdown menu.
3. Once downloaded, extract/unzip the file on your computer.

### Step 2: Upload to Gemini
1. Open your Gemini app interface and click on **Customise** in the left sidebar.
2. Select the **Skills** tab at the top of the screen.
3. Click the **Create** button and choose **Upload skill**.
4. Select or drag the extracted folder named `life-copilot` (located inside `agents/skills/`) straight into the upload box.

## 🎯 How to Use It
The skill wakes up completely automatically when you type inside your normal Gemini chat window. Try pasting things like:

* "I'm totally overwhelmed today, here is everything I need to do: [paste list]"
* "Clean up this voice text I just recorded while walking"
* "Help me write an awkward email to my landlord about a broken sink"
* "Should I buy a used car or lease a new one? Help me decide"
