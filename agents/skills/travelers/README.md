# ✈️ Travelers

For anyone with a trip on the horizon, from a once-in-a-lifetime holiday to a spontaneous Saturday out. Plan smarter itineraries, pack lighter, spend wisely and connect with locals.

## Skills in this pack

| Skill | What it does | Try saying |
|---|---|---|
| [`trip-planner`](./trip-planner/SKILL.md) | Day-by-day itineraries balancing sights, food, rest and realistic travel time | "Plan 5 relaxed days in Lisbon for two foodies in May" |
| [`packing-pro`](./packing-pro/SKILL.md) | Tailored packing lists by weather, length and activities, carry-on or checked | "What should I pack for 10 days in Iceland in October, carry-on only?" |
| [`travel-budgeter`](./travel-budgeter/SKILL.md) | Categorised trip cost estimates, a daily spending limit and money-saving tactics | "I have 1500 for a week in Thailand. Is that enough and what's my daily budget?" |
| [`phrasebook-buddy`](./phrasebook-buddy/SKILL.md) | Essential phrases with easy pronunciation, etiquette tips and emergency phrases | "Give me the basic Japanese phrases and manners I need for my first trip" |
| [`local-explorer`](./local-explorer/SKILL.md) | Weekend and day-trip ideas near home with a ready-to-go plan | "Something fun and cheap to do within an hour of Manchester this Sunday with the kids" |

## How to install

Upload any skill folder from this pack (for example `trip-planner/`) using the steps in the [main README](../../../README.md#-how-to-install).
