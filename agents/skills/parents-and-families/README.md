# 👨‍👩‍👧 Parents, Families & Home

For busy parents, carers and anyone running a household. These skills take the mental load off meals, chores, kids, pets, repairs and big family moments, so home feels calmer and more organised.

## Skills in this pack

| Skill | What it does | Try saying |
|---|---|---|
| [`grocery-list-genius`](./grocery-list-genius/SKILL.md) | Turns recipes and meal ideas into one aisle-ordered list with quantities | "Make a shopping list for spaghetti bolognese, chicken fajitas and a veggie curry for 4" |
| [`pantry-chef`](./pantry-chef/SKILL.md) | Cooks real meals from what's already in your fridge and cupboards | "I have eggs, half a broccoli, rice and a tin of tuna. What's for dinner?" |
| [`cleaning-schedule`](./cleaning-schedule/SKILL.md) | Builds a realistic chore routine split fairly across the household | "Make a cleaning schedule for our 3-bed house, 2 adults and kids aged 6 and 10" |
| [`declutter-coach`](./declutter-coach/SKILL.md) | Coaches room-by-room decluttering with fast keep, donate or bin decisions | "My spare room is a dumping ground and I don't know where to start" |
| [`moving-checklist`](./moving-checklist/SKILL.md) | Week-by-week moving plan, packing order, address changes and first-night box | "We're moving house on 15 March with two kids and a dog. Make me a plan" |
| [`kids-activity-ideas`](./kids-activity-ideas/SKILL.md) | Age-appropriate, low-cost activities with materials, time and safety notes | "Rainy Saturday, a 4-year-old and a 7-year-old, no screens. Help!" |
| [`calm-parent`](./calm-parent/SKILL.md) | Calm, age-aware scripts for tantrums, bedtime, screens and sibling fights | "My 3-year-old screams every time we leave the park. What do I say?" |
| [`pet-care-planner`](./pet-care-planner/SKILL.md) | Daily-to-yearly pet routines, vet reminders and a new-pet checklist | "We're bringing home an 8-week-old Labrador puppy next week" |
| [`diy-fix-it`](./diy-fix-it/SKILL.md) | Step-by-step home fixes with tools list, safety rating and when to call a pro | "My kitchen tap keeps dripping even when it's turned off" |
| [`party-planner`](./party-planner/SKILL.md) | Party plans with timeline, food quantities, budget split and day-of checklist | "Plan a dinosaur party for my son's 6th birthday, 12 kids, budget 200" |

## How to install

Upload any skill folder from this pack (for example `grocery-list-genius/`) using the steps in the [main README](../../../README.md#-how-to-install).
