# 💼 Professionals & Job Seekers

For anyone landing a job, growing in one, or changing direction entirely. These skills help you tell your story, negotiate your worth, handle the hard conversations and walk into every new role with a plan.

## Skills in this pack

| Skill | What it does | Try saying |
|---|---|---|
| [`career-catalyst`](./career-catalyst/SKILL.md) | Sharpens resumes, cover letters and LinkedIn profiles, plus mock interview practice | "Rewrite my resume bullets for this product manager job description" |
| [`star-story-bank`](./star-story-bank/SKILL.md) | Builds reusable STAR interview stories mapped to the competencies employers test | "Turn my last three projects into STAR stories for behavioral interviews" |
| [`salary-negotiator`](./salary-negotiator/SKILL.md) | Sets your anchor and walk-away, then scripts every pushback reply | "They offered me 72k and I want 85k - what do I say?" |
| [`personal-brand-builder`](./personal-brand-builder/SKILL.md) | Writes LinkedIn posts and bios with strong hooks and a posting plan | "Write a LinkedIn post about the mistake that taught me to delegate" |
| [`career-switch-navigator`](./career-switch-navigator/SKILL.md) | Maps transferable skills, closes gaps and plots your route into a new field | "I'm a teacher and want to move into UX design - where do I start?" |
| [`performance-review-writer`](./performance-review-writer/SKILL.md) | Turns a year of work into evidence-based reviews and sharp next-cycle goals | "Help me write my self-review - here's everything I did this year" |
| [`workplace-diplomat`](./workplace-diplomat/SKILL.md) | Scripts hard work conversations with SBI feedback, calm no's and de-escalation | "How do I tell my manager this deadline isn't realistic?" |
| [`first-90-days`](./first-90-days/SKILL.md) | Builds a 30-60-90 plan with stakeholder map, quick wins and check-ins | "I start as a team lead on Monday - make me a 90-day plan" |
| [`job-offer-evaluator`](./job-offer-evaluator/SKILL.md) | Compares offers on real total pay, growth, culture and hidden risks | "Compare these two offers - one is remote, one has equity" |
| [`meeting-minutes`](./meeting-minutes/SKILL.md) | Turns messy notes into decisions, owned actions and a follow-up email | "Here's the transcript from today's call - write up the minutes" |

## How to install

Upload any skill folder from this pack (for example `career-catalyst/`) using the steps in the [main README](../../../README.md#-how-to-install).
