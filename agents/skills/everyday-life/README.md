# ⚡ Everyday Life: Productivity & Focus

For anyone juggling work, life and a never-ending to-do list. Focus deeper, decide faster, build better habits and finally get your inbox and notes under control.

## Skills in this pack

| Skill | What it does | Try saying |
|---|---|---|
| [`life-copilot`](./life-copilot/SKILL.md) | All-round helper that turns messy thoughts, tasks and text into clear, scannable plans | "Here's my brain dump for today - turn it into a plan" |
| [`focus-sprint`](./focus-sprint/SKILL.md) | One-priority deep-work session with timed sprints, a distraction shield and check-in | "I have 2 hours to finish my report and I keep getting distracted" |
| [`inbox-zero`](./inbox-zero/SKILL.md) | Sorts your messages into reply, delegate, schedule or archive and drafts quick replies | "Triage these 15 emails and draft replies to the quick ones" |
| [`weekly-review`](./weekly-review/SKILL.md) | Reflect on wins and misses, decide carry-overs, and time-block next week's top 3 | "Let's do my Sunday weekly review - here's what happened this week" |
| [`goal-architect`](./goal-architect/SKILL.md) | Turns vague ambitions into SMART goals or OKRs with milestones and a first step | "I want to get fit this year - make it a real goal with a plan" |
| [`habit-builder`](./habit-builder/SKILL.md) | Tiny, stackable habits with cues, rewards, a tracker and a missed-day plan | "Help me build a daily reading habit that actually sticks" |
| [`procrastination-breaker`](./procrastination-breaker/SKILL.md) | Finds why you're stuck and gets you started in five minutes, guilt-free | "I've been avoiding my tax return for three weeks" |
| [`decision-compass`](./decision-compass/SKILL.md) | Weighted decision matrix with a sensitivity check and regret-minimisation gut check | "Help me choose between three job offers - here's what matters to me" |
| [`second-brain`](./second-brain/SKILL.md) | Organises notes and ideas with PARA, naming rules, templates and a review routine | "My Notion is a mess - set up a second brain I'll actually use" |

## How to install

Upload any skill folder from this pack (for example `life-copilot/`) using the steps in the [main README](../../../README.md#-how-to-install).
