# ✍️ Writers & Communicators

For anyone who wants their words to land, from a tricky text to a wedding toast. Get the tone right, the grammar clean and the message clear every time.

## Skills in this pack

| Skill | What it does | Try saying |
|---|---|---|
| [`tone-tuner`](./tone-tuner/SKILL.md) | Rewrites your message in the tone you need, with 2-3 ready-to-send variants | "Make this email to my landlord sound firmer but still polite" |
| [`grammar-guardian`](./grammar-guardian/SKILL.md) | Clean, proofread text plus a short table of the key fixes and why | "Proofread my cover letter before I send it - UK English please" |
| [`reply-wizard`](./reply-wizard/SKILL.md) | Three replies from warm to firm for awkward or emotional messages | "My friend keeps asking to borrow money again - how do I say no without ruining things?" |
| [`summary-surgeon`](./summary-surgeon/SKILL.md) | TL;DR, ranked key points, exact numbers and what it means for you | "Summarise this 30-page report - I'm on the finance team" |
| [`plain-english`](./plain-english/SKILL.md) | Translates legal, medical or technical jargon into plain words, meaning intact | "What does this clause in my tenancy agreement actually mean?" |
| [`speech-writer`](./speech-writer/SKILL.md) | Speeches and toasts timed to your slot and built from your own stories | "Write a 4-minute best man speech - here are three stories about Sam" |
| [`card-message-writer`](./card-message-writer/SKILL.md) | Heartfelt card messages in several lengths and tones for any occasion | "What do I write in a sympathy card for a colleague who lost her dad?" |

## How to install

Upload any skill folder from this pack (for example `tone-tuner/`) using the steps in the [main README](../../../README.md#-how-to-install).
