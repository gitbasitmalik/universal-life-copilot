# 💰 Personal Finance

For anyone who wants to feel in control of their money: budget, save, pay off debt and spend smarter, without jargon or judgement. Plain-language money education, never personalised financial advice.

## Skills in this pack

| Skill | What it does | Try saying |
|---|---|---|
| [`budget-builder`](./budget-builder/SKILL.md) | Clear monthly budget with 50/30/20 or zero-based method and fixes | "I take home 2,800 a month and always run out before payday" |
| [`expense-sorter`](./expense-sorter/SKILL.md) | Sorts pasted transactions into categories and spots your money leaks | "Here's last month's spending export, where did my money go?" |
| [`debt-payoff-planner`](./debt-payoff-planner/SKILL.md) | Avalanche vs snowball comparison, payoff order and debt-free milestones | "I have two credit cards and a car loan, which do I pay first?" |
| [`savings-goal-planner`](./savings-goal-planner/SKILL.md) | Monthly savings amounts, timelines and automation for every goal | "How much do I need to save each month for a 3,000 trip next August?" |
| [`subscription-auditor`](./subscription-auditor/SKILL.md) | Finds the true yearly cost of subscriptions and what to cancel | "Here are all my subscriptions, what should I cut?" |
| [`bill-negotiator`](./bill-negotiator/SKILL.md) | Research checklist and word-for-word scripts to lower your bills | "My internet bill just went up 20%, give me a script to call them" |
| [`smart-shopper`](./smart-shopper/SKILL.md) | Cost-per-use maths and comparison tables to beat impulse buys | "Should I buy the 900 laptop or the 1,400 one for uni?" |
| [`tax-doc-organizer`](./tax-doc-organizer/SKILL.md) | Tax document checklist, folder system and deadline tracker | "I'm freelance for the first time, what paperwork do I need for taxes?" |
| [`money-basics-tutor`](./money-basics-tutor/SKILL.md) | Plain-English money concepts with simple worked examples | "Explain compound interest like I'm completely new to this" |

## How to install

Upload any skill folder from this pack (for example `budget-builder/`) using the steps in the [main README](../../../README.md#-how-to-install).
