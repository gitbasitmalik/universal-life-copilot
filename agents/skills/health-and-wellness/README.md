# 🌿 Health & Wellness

For anyone who wants to eat better, move more, sleep deeper and feel calmer, with small realistic steps instead of extreme overhauls. Friendly everyday guidance, never a replacement for a doctor.

## Skills in this pack

| Skill | What it does | Try saying |
|---|---|---|
| [`meal-planner`](./meal-planner/SKILL.md) | Weekly meal plans that fit your budget, diet and time, plus grocery list | "Plan a week of cheap high-protein dinners for two, 30 minutes max" |
| [`workout-builder`](./workout-builder/SKILL.md) | Gym or home workout plans with sets, reps, progression and form cues | "Make me a 3-day beginner plan with just dumbbells at home" |
| [`sleep-reset`](./sleep-reset/SKILL.md) | Wind-down routine, steady schedule and a 2-week sleep diary to fix sleep | "I can't fall asleep before 2am and have to be up at 7" |
| [`stress-unwind`](./stress-unwind/SKILL.md) | Calm down fast with breathing and grounding, then sort the worry | "I'm panicking about my presentation in an hour, help me calm down" |
| [`mood-journal`](./mood-journal/SKILL.md) | Guided prompts, mood tracking and gentle reframing of harsh thoughts | "Give me a journal prompt, I've had a rough day at work" |
| [`healthy-swaps`](./healthy-swaps/SKILL.md) | Lighter versions of favourite recipes and snacks that still taste great | "Make my mac and cheese recipe healthier without ruining it" |
| [`doctor-visit-prep`](./doctor-visit-prep/SKILL.md) | Symptom timeline, top questions and medication list for your appointment | "Help me prepare for my GP appointment about headaches tomorrow" |
| [`meditation-guide`](./meditation-guide/SKILL.md) | Guided meditation scripts of any length, paced for reading aloud | "Write a 10-minute body scan meditation for falling asleep" |
| [`couch-to-5k`](./couch-to-5k/SKILL.md) | Beginner run/walk plan to your first 5K, injury-free and motivating | "I can barely run a minute, get me to 5K by spring" |
| [`digital-detox`](./digital-detox/SKILL.md) | Screen-time audit, phone-free zones and a 7-day reset challenge | "I spend 6 hours a day on my phone and want to cut it in half" |

## How to install

Upload any skill folder from this pack (for example `meal-planner/`) using the steps in the [main README](../../../README.md#-how-to-install).
