# 🎓 Students & Lifelong Learners

For anyone with an exam, an essay or a new skill on the horizon. These skills turn messy notes into real understanding, build plans you can stick to, and coach you to do the work yourself so it sticks.

## Skills in this pack

| Skill | What it does | Try saying |
|---|---|---|
| [`study-buddy`](./study-buddy/SKILL.md) | Turns notes into key concepts, layered explanations, self-tests and a review plan | "Summarise this biology chapter and quiz me on it" |
| [`flashcard-forge`](./flashcard-forge/SKILL.md) | Makes one-fact flashcards ready to import into Anki or Quizlet | "Turn these history notes into Anki cards" |
| [`socratic-tutor`](./socratic-tutor/SKILL.md) | Guides you to the answer with questions, never just handing it over | "Tutor me on why the sky is blue, don't just tell me" |
| [`essay-coach`](./essay-coach/SKILL.md) | Sharpens your thesis, outline and PEEL paragraphs with marker-style feedback | "Is my thesis strong enough for this essay question?" |
| [`exam-cram-planner`](./exam-cram-planner/SKILL.md) | Builds a revision timetable backwards from exam dates, weak topics first | "I have 4 exams in 3 weeks, make me a revision plan" |
| [`language-partner`](./language-partner/SKILL.md) | Level-matched conversation practice with gentle corrections and vocab lists | "Let's role-play ordering at a cafe in Spanish, I'm A2" |
| [`math-step-solver`](./math-step-solver/SKILL.md) | Solves maths step by step, checks the answer and gives practice | "Solve 2x^2 + 3x - 5 = 0 and show every step" |
| [`citation-helper`](./citation-helper/SKILL.md) | Formats APA, MLA, Harvard or Chicago citations without inventing sources | "Format these three sources in APA 7 with in-text citations" |
| [`book-digest`](./book-digest/SKILL.md) | Book notes with key ideas, takeaways and book-club discussion questions | "Give me key takeaways and club questions for Atomic Habits" |
| [`skill-roadmap`](./skill-roadmap/SKILL.md) | Staged learning roadmap with milestones, weekly practice and progress checks | "I want to learn guitar in 6 months with 3 hours a week" |

## How to install

Upload any skill folder from this pack (for example `study-buddy/`) using the steps in the [main README](../../../README.md#-how-to-install).
